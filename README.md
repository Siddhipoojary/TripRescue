TripRescue — AI-Powered Travel Disruption Recovery

Problem:

One travel delay can disrupt an entire journey, affecting connecting transport, hotel check-ins, and important commitments such as interviews. Travellers often have to resolve every issue manually under stress.

Solution:

TripRescue is an AI-powered, multi-agent travel recovery assistant that identifies disruptions, maps affected bookings, and recommends recovery plans based on budget, deadlines, preferences, and accessibility needs.

Key Features:

Disruption Detection: Identifies travel disruptions and potential booking impacts.

Domino Map: Shows how one disruption affects the rest of the journey.

Recovery Planner: Compares fastest, cheapest, and least stressful options.

RescueScore: Scores recovery plans based on time, cost, risk, transfers, and preferences.

Accessibility Support: Considers mobility needs, assistance requirements, and comfort.

Voice Assistant: Allows travellers to describe disruptions and hear recovery options.

What-If Slider: Explores how different delay durations affect recovery plans.

Approval-Based Actions: Keeps the traveller in control before consequential actions.
